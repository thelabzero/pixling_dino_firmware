Before you start (about 5 minutes)
  1. Flash the stream firmware and close every serial monitor: cd firmware && pio run -e imu_calibrator -t upload
  2. Use Chrome or Edge, press Connect and pick the ESP32-C3 port. The board messages should show MPU at 0x68. No hardware? Press Demo to rehearse.
  3. Let the board run for 1–2 minutes first: the gyro drifts while the chip warms up.
  4. Work on a solid, level desk. Don't touch the USB cable or the desk during a capture: the bar under the steps shows progress, and the page rejects a capture if you moved.
  5. Do the steps in order 1, 2, 3. The blue banner always says what to do next. If you redo step 2, step 3 is cleared and must be redone (it depends on step 2).
  6. At the end press Copy for config.h and paste it over the IMU_* lines in firmware/include/config.h, then re-flash the normal firmware.

Connect the board (or press Demo) to begin.

1 · Gyro bias

Why: a still gyro should read 0 °/s; its error is the bias. Do: set the board flat on the desk, hands off, wait 1–2 min after power-on, then press the button. 3 s. The firmware keeps re-learning the bias while the dino sits still, so this is only its starting value.

not done

2 · Accelerometer, 6 positions

Why: gravity is exactly 1 g, so each face-up pose shows how wrong each axis is (offset and scale). Do: rest the board on each of its 6 faces, one at a time, as square as you can (prop it against a book or box corner so it can't lean or roll), wait for the hint below to name the face, press that button and keep still for 2 s. Suggested order: Z, then X, then Y, up and down.

Connect the board to get live hints.

Good result: every face within 5° of vertical. Which sign (+/−) a physical side gets depends on how the chip is soldered, so trust the hint and the Live accel numbers, not a guess.

0 / 6

3 · Mounting (optional)

Why: the chip may be turned or tilted inside the case; this tells the firmware where "up" and "the screen side" really are, so tilt and flip work. Do (a): stand the dino exactly as it sits in use (upright, screen towards you) and press a). Then (b): lay it flat on its screen (screen down on the table, not on an edge) and press b). 1.5 s each, in this order. Needs step 2 first.

After pasting: check it (2 minutes)
  • This page, right now (needs step 3): device tilt must read about 0 when the dino stands upright; tilt the top to the right and it must go +; lean it back and device lean goes +; lay it on its screen and face must read down. If tilt goes the wrong way, redo step 3 (b).
  • Quality box: |accel| within ±0.010 g in every pose and gyro within ±0.3 °/s at rest.
  • On the device: flash dino_c3, press i in the monitor, then tilt left and right and flip it face-down: the readings and the dino should follow.
  • Save the numbers with Save JSON (store in tools/imu_calibrator/results/) so there is a record. Re-calibrate whenever the IMU is re-mounted.
Something wrong?
  • No data after Connect: a serial monitor is still open, or the board has the other firmware. Close monitors, re-flash imu_calibrator, press Rescan.
  • "Moved during capture": don't touch the desk or cable; put the board on something solid and retry.
  • "That doesn't look like +Y up": a different axis is pointing up. Read the Live accel numbers: the axis near +1 or −1 is the one to press.
  • "N° off vertical" (above 5°): the face wasn't square; prop it better and redo that pose.
  • |accel| error above 20 mg after step 2: poses weren't still or square. Press Clear and redo all six slowly.
  • Big offsets (above 0.1 g) are normal for cheap modules; what matters is the quality box afterwards.
  • Left and right swapped in the firmware: redo step 3, making sure (a) is upright and (b) is screen-down.

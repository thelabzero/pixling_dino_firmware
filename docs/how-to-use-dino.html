How to use dino

A small desk dinosaur that lives entirely in its face. This page covers what the current firmware does and how to drive it.

Power onControlsDino's dayMenu AlarmFocusStopwatchSerial keysHelp

1. Power on

  1. Plug dino in with a USB-C data cable (or connect its battery). The face appears on the screen.
  2. On start it joins your Wi-Fi once to get the time, then again about once a day. This is the only network use; there is no camera, microphone or cloud.
  3. Leave it on its home screen: dino idles, blinks and has small quirks.
No Wi-Fi, no time. If the time was never synced the clock shows --:-- and alarms will not ring. Dino still reacts to touch and motion. Setup steps are in the project README.

2. The five ways to talk to dino

Touch pad plus motion. The same gesture does different things depending on the screen.

Holding a tilt repeats the move every 0.7 s, so you can scroll through values by keeping it tilted.

3. Dino's day (home screen)

When no menu or timer is open, dino lives on its own. It never begs for attention or nags.

What you do / what happensWhat dino does

TapPet reaction, starting 0.12 to 0.3 s after the touch
Tap 4 times within 8 sOver-petted reaction, then for 10 s further taps only get a glance
Shake itShaken reaction

Dino also makes short piezo sounds for its voices, the menu and the alarm. Sound is silent only if the buzzer is disabled in the firmware.

4. The menu

Long press from the home screen. The menu has five cards. It closes by itself after 10 s without input.

  1. Tilt left or right to move to the next card.
  2. Tap to choose the card.
  3. Long press (or choose Back) to leave.
CardWhat it does
ClockTurns clock mode on or off. When on, the time stays on the home screen. The picture moves 1 px every 3 minutes to protect the OLED from burn-in.
AlarmSet the alarm (section 5)
FocusStart a focus session (section 6)
StopwatchOpen the stopwatch (section 7)
BackReturn to the home screen

5. Alarm

Set it

  1. Long press, tilt to Alarm, tap. The time board opens at the current time with the hours blinking.
  2. Tilt right or left to add or subtract 1 hour.
  3. Tap to move to the minutes (blinking).
  4. Tilt to change minutes in steps of 5.
  5. Tap to save. Dino shows how long until it rings (for example +07:30) and goes home.
  • Hold on the set-alarm screen turns the alarm off.
  • Long press leaves without changing anything.
  • The alarm is stored in flash, so it survives a power cut.

When it rings

DoResult
TapSnooze: rings again after 5 minutes
Hold, or flip face-downDismiss. Dino wakes up
Do nothingThe sound escalates through three stages, 20 s each, and stops by itself after 10 minutes
Alarms follow the real clock. They never wait for an animation to finish.

6. Focus session

  1. Long press, tilt to Focus, tap.
  2. Tilt to choose 25, 45 or 60 minutes.
  3. Tap, or flip dino face-down, to start. Dino reads while the minutes count down on screen.
  4. When the time is up dino celebrates, then a 5 minute break follows.
  • Long press during focus gives up and returns home.
  • Tap or long press during the break skips it.

7. Stopwatch

  • Menu, Stopwatch, tap. It shows MM:SS.
  • Tap pauses or resumes. Dino marks each full minute with a small animation and a sound.
  • Long press leaves the stopwatch.

Time is off by hoursThe time zone setting in the firmware is wrong
Tilt moves the wrong way or too easilyThe motion sensor needs calibrating for this unit (see the IMU calibration guide)

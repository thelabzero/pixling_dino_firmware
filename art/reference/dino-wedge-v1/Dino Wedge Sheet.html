Dino, wedge eyes
The same dino with the new D · Wedge face: flat-cut wedge eyes and a small mouth, with no pupil, no brow and no nose. The top edge of each eye tilts to set the mood. Every clip of the v1 sheet (face rig, life, utility, games, friends) is redrawn with this face; icons, type and props are unchanged. Every frame is a real 128 × 64 1-bit bitmap. The large preview plays live; the small one under it is 1:1. Click any frame to pin it in the large preview. Assumption: the 8 presets that are not on the new-eyes page (unimpressed, focus, hungry, chew, scared, determined, squeeze, yawn) are my own wedge versions, for review.

Constraint notes
Face scale. Full face is 1.0: eyes 18 × 14 at ±16 px from centre, mouth 8 px under the eyes. Utility screens use 0.55 (eyes about 10 × 8) at the left. Nothing goes below 0.55, where a slant is still two or three pixels.
Eye cuts. Mood is carried by the top edge: slant in (mischief, grumpy, rawr), slant out (sad), a flat half lid (smug, unimpressed), a bar (sleepy, blink). Whole-eye shapes: arch, heart, star, spiral.
Text. 5 × 7 uppercase only, one line of 10 characters at 1×, or 6 characters at 2× for received messages. Digits use the 9 × 13 and 4 × 7 sets.
Burn-in. Clock, stopwatch and focus pass the whole frame through a 3 × 3 offset ring, one 1 px step every 3 minutes.
Flashing. Nothing inverts or strobes. The only blinking element is the 20 × 2 px set-alarm underline at 1.25 Hz.
